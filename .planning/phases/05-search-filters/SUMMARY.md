# Phase 5 Summary: Search, Categories, Filters & Sorting

## Completed Deliverables
- **Search Engine**: Instant client-side search across name, description, category, tags, and use cases (`filterTools.ts`).
- **Category Navigation**: 10 functional categories with accurate dynamic item count badges (`FilterBar.tsx`).
- **Granular Controls**: Filters for verified pricing tiers, 5 curated collections, and 3 sorting modes (Featured, Alphabetical, Recently Added).
- **Clear Actions & Count**: Live results counter ("Showing X of Y tools"), active filter pills, and "Clear filters" action.
- **Empty States**: Customized empty states for empty search queries, combined filter misses, and empty favorites (`EmptyState.tsx`).
